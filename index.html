Emotion Recognition from Speech Acoustics for Human-Robot Interaction

A Speech Emotion Recognition project that investigates how emotion can be identified from human speech to support more natural and emotion-aware interaction between humans and robots.

Presented by Donia Al-Barrak Shomoukh Al-Otaibi
Supervisor Dr. Muath Al-Suhaibani

About the Project

Human speech carries more than words; it also contains acoustic information related to emotion. This project focuses on Speech Emotion Recognition (SER), where speech recordings are processed and analyzed by trained models to classify the speaker's emotion.

Current emotion classes

The current system focuses on four classes: Happy, Sad, Angry, and Neutral. The project is still under development and the final model and evaluation will be updated as experiments are completed.

How It Works
